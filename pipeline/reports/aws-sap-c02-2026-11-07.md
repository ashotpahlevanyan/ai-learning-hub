- Fixed:
  - Cost allocation tags: lesson d1-l5 (section "Attribution building blocks", keyPoint 1 and gotcha) and flashcard d1-f29 now say tags "by default apply from activation onward; activate early". The old wording said tags are strictly not retroactive.

  - d1-q11 relies on RCP coverage of KMS. RCPs do not apply to AWS managed KMS keys. The scenario implies customer managed keys but does not say so.

**d2 (39 questions)**
- Fixed:

  - d2-q1 rationale 3: no longer claims that API Gateway cannot split traffic by percentage (canary releases can).
  - d2-q22 rationale 0: the "over the internet" claim about NAT gateway traffic was softened.
